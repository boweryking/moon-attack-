# Creature vs Caterpillars - Defense Game

A fast-paced action game where you play as a cute creature defending your territory from waves of invading caterpillars!

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Click "START GAME" to begin
3. Use **Arrow Keys** (← →) or **Joystick** (mouse/touch) to move your creature left and right
4. Press **SHIFT** to spit at caterpillars
5. Survive waves of increasingly difficult caterpillar attacks
6. Don't let your health reach zero!

## Features

- **Wave System**: Each wave brings more caterpillars
- **Health System**: Start with 100 health, lose 10 per hit, lose 50 if a caterpillar crashes into you
- **Scoring**: Earn 10 points per caterpillar destroyed
- **Dual Controls**: Keyboard arrows OR virtual joystick (touch/mouse)
- **Enemy AI**: Caterpillars wiggle as they move horizontally and spit back at you
- **Visual Effects**: Particle explosions, glowing effects, and animated starfield
- **Responsive Controls**: Smooth creature movement with joystick support

## Game Mechanics

- **Player**: Pink creature that spits cyan projectiles upward
- **Enemies**: Green wiggling caterpillars that spit green projectiles
- **Collision**: Spit destroys caterpillars, caterpillar spit damages you
- **Progression**: Waves get harder with more caterpillars spawning

## Controls

- **Keyboard**: Arrow keys to move, Shift to spit
- **Joystick**: Click/touch and drag anywhere on the canvas to move, Shift to spit
- **Touch**: Works on mobile devices with touch joystick

## Technologies

- HTML5 Canvas for rendering
- Pure JavaScript for game logic
- CSS3 for UI styling and animations
- Touch and mouse event handling for joystick controls

Enjoy defending your territory!
