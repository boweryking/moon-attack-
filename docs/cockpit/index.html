Cockpit Asteroids

First-person space fighter. Hunt the orange jets that run, dodge the pink jets that hunt you, and watch for rocks. Trackpad and keys on a laptop, thumbs on an iPad.

AimKeep the cursor inside the dashed box to aim without turning. Click, or hold Shift, to fire. With auto-fire on, the guns open up the moment the crosshair sits on a rock.
Turn and thrustPush the cursor out past the box and the ship turns that way, faster the further out. Hold ↑ to thrust the way you are facing and ↓ to brake. Turn with the trackpad while you thrust.
JetsPink jets fly at you and fire. Orange jets run, so chase them down. Each takes two hits and the crosshair bracket leads the shot for you. Press X to spin around fast. Pink and orange arrows on the edge point to jets you can't see.
Onboard computerPress H or tap ? any time for the help screen. The ship also talks: it warns you about hostiles behind you, rocks close by, low hull and shields. Target with T, throw a grenade with B, hold Space for the shield.
On an iPadTap Tilt steering on the page, then steer by tilting the iPad (needs the hosted page, see below). Or drag on the left half to steer with a floating stick. The lever on the right edge sets your thrust and stays where you leave it. FIRE sits in the middle, SHIELD and BOMB on the left, TARGET and 180 on the right.
WarningsA red pulsing arrow means a rock is close and out of sight in that direction. Turn toward the arrow to see it. The scope below the crosshair shows every rock around you, ahead at the top.
