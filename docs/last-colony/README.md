# Last Colony

A Defender-style arcade game. You are the Guardian, flying a jet over the colony world Haven, shooting Harvester landers before they carry colonists off.

- **Keys:** Up/Down move, Left/Right turn and thrust, Space fires, B smart bomb, H warp, X turn around, P pause, M mute, C toggles CRT effects.
- **Phone:** hold sideways. Left thumb: joystick halfway up the screen (push sideways to fly that way, up or down to climb and dive; harder push = faster). Right thumb: fire (hold), smart bomb, warp.
- **Colonists:** shoot a lander that is carrying one, catch the falling colonist, and set them on the ground for a bonus. Lose them all and the planet explodes and the landers become mutants.
- **Enemies:** landers, mutants, bombers (drop mines), pods (burst into swarmers), baiters (appear if you take too long).
- Audio is in `audio/` (ElevenLabs): `sfx/`, `voice/`, `music/`.

- **Voice commands** (press V, needs Chrome, Edge or Safari on the real website, not in the claude.ai preview): say "bomb", "warp", "turn", "pause", "resume", "mute", "start".
- Switching to another window pauses the game; press P or click to resume. Option/Command/Control are ignored if you hit them by accident.
