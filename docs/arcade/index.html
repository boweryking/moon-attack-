On a laptop

Click a game and play. Most use the mouse or trackpad plus a few keys. In Asteroids, Shift fires, Space raises the shield and V turns on voice commands.

On a phone

Hold it sideways for most games, or upright for Spore Siege. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from there to hide the browser bars.

On an iPad

Cockpit Asteroids can steer by tilting the iPad. Tap Tilt steering under the game and allow motion when asked.

Games are made by hand and still changing. Tell us what is too hard, too small or too slow.
