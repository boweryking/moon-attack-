# Suno prompts for Asteroids

Paste each into Suno's **Style** box and turn on **Instrumental**.
Add "seamless loop, no fade out" if you want it to loop cleanly. Generate 2-3 takes and keep the one whose start and end match best.

## 1. Classic Arcade Pulse (waves 1-3, faithful to the original)
Minimalist 8-bit chiptune, ominous two-note bass heartbeat, sparse square-wave arpeggios, cold and tense, 1979 vector arcade cabinet vibe, slow build, loopable, instrumental

## 2. Neon Synthwave (main gameplay)
Dark synthwave, pulsing analog bass, shimmering arpeggiated synths, gated reverb snares, 120 BPM, neon retro-futuristic space chase, driving and cinematic, loopable, instrumental

## 3. Deep Space Ambient (between waves)
Dark ambient space drone, slow evolving pads, distant sonar pings, subtle sub-bass pulse, vast and lonely, cosmic dread, no drums, instrumental

## 4. Industrial Boss Wave (wave 6 and up, saucers)
Aggressive industrial electronic, distorted bass, hard four-on-the-floor kick, glitchy percussion, alarm-like synth stabs, 140 BPM, relentless and menacing, instrumental

## 5. Epic Orchestral-Electronic (title screen / story intro)
Cinematic sci-fi orchestral with electronic pulses, low brass swells, ticking synth rhythm, rising tension, heroic final-stand energy, instrumental

## 6. Stingers
Game over (about 10 s):
Short 10-second retro game-over jingle, descending 8-bit melody, sad and dramatic, ends on a low note, instrumental

Victory (about 8 s):
Short 8-second triumphant chiptune victory fanfare, bright rising arpeggio, instrumental

## Suggested file names (put in asteroids/audio/music/)
title.mp3, wave_early.mp3, gameplay.mp3, between_waves.mp3, boss.mp3, gameover.mp3, victory.mp3
